Privacy Policy
--------------

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RC Flight and Battery Log (“the App”) handles information when you use the App.

1. Information stored by the App
The App allows you to create and store RC model flight records. Depending on how you use the App, the following information may be stored:
	•	RC model details, such as model name and other information you enter about your model.
	•	Flight logs and associated flight information.
	•	GPS location information used to record the location of a flight.
	•	Photographs that you choose to associate with RC models or flight logs.
	•	Pilot names that you enter into the App.
This information is provided by you or generated by the App as part of recording your flights.

2. How your data is stored
Your data is primarily stored locally on your Android device.
The App does not operate its own cloud storage or server for your flight data.
You may optionally use the App's Dropbox backup functionality to create a backup of your data. If you choose to use this feature, the App transfers the backup data to and from your own Dropbox account.
Dropbox is a third-party service and its use is subject to Dropbox's own terms and privacy policy.

3. How your data is used
The data stored by the App is used solely to provide the App's functionality, including:
	•	Recording and managing RC model flight logs.
	•	Recording flight locations using GPS.
	•	Associating photographs with models or flight logs.
	•	Recording pilot information.
	•	Creating and restoring backups using Dropbox when you choose to use the backup functionality.
The developer does not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or selling information.

4. GPS and location information
The App may use your device's GPS/location services to record the location of an RC flight.
Location information is stored as part of your flight log on your device. It is not collected by the developer or transmitted to the developer's servers.
If you choose to back up your flight data using Dropbox, the location information contained in your backup will also be transferred to your Dropbox account.
You can control the App's access to location services through the Android device's permission settings. Disabling location permission may prevent the App from recording flight locations.

5. Photos
The App allows you to associate photographs with RC models and flight logs.
Photos that you add to the App are stored as part of your App data on your device. They are not transmitted to the developer.
If you choose to back up your App data using Dropbox, photographs included in the backup will also be transferred to your Dropbox account.

6. Data security
The App is designed to keep your personal and sensitive information under your control.
Data stored by the App remains on your Android device unless you choose to use the Dropbox backup functionality. The security of locally stored data is also dependent on the security measures you use to protect your Android device, including your device lock and Android's built-in security features.
When data is transferred between the App and Dropbox for backup or restore purposes, the transfer is made using secure communication provided by the Dropbox service.
The App does not transmit your data to the developer's own servers.
Access to your Dropbox account is handled through Dropbox's authentication and authorisation mechanisms. The App does not receive or store your Dropbox account password.
You should use appropriate security measures on your device and Dropbox account to protect your data from unauthorised access.

7. Data sharing
The developer does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.
The only external service used by the App for transferring user data is Dropbox, and this occurs only when you choose to use the Dropbox backup or restore functionality.
When you use Dropbox backup, your data is transferred to your Dropbox account and is subject to Dropbox's privacy practices and terms.

8. Data retention and deletion
Your App data remains on your device until you choose to modify or delete it.
The App provides functionality allowing you to delete your stored data from the device.
If you create a backup using Dropbox, a copy of your data may also remain in your Dropbox account. You can manage and delete those backups through your Dropbox account.
The developer does not retain a copy of your App data on its own servers.

9. Children's privacy
The App is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.
The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not operate its own user accounts or collect data on its own servers, the developer does not have direct access to determine the age of users.

10. Third-party services
The App does not contain advertising and does not use advertising networks.
The App does not use third-party analytics services to collect information about your use of the App.
The App may integrate with Dropbox solely to provide optional backup and restore functionality.
Use of Dropbox is subject to Dropbox's own terms and privacy policy.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable legal requirements.
Any updated version will be made available at the location where this Privacy Policy is published.

12. Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the way the App handles your data, please contact the developer using the contact details provided on the App's Google Play Store listing.
